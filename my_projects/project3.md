### Project Overview
This academic project applies time series forecasting to historical US retail sales data (1992–2021), highlighting the impacts of COVID-19 on trends and recovery patterns.

#### Key Insights
- ARIMA model captured post-2020 recovery trends.  
- Seasonal patterns showed predictable holiday spikes.  
- RMSE indicates reliable short-term predictions for retail planning.
